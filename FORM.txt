=== What are you building, and who is it for? (max 1000) ===
Mulligan is a perpetuals venue on HyperEVM where a losing trade buys you a piece of the house. Traders trade BTC, ETH, SOL and HYPE perps against a shared house, priced directly from HyperCore's oracle and mark precompiles. The house is the MULL token: LPs mint it at NAV, it earns every fee and trading loss, and it pays every winner. Close a losing position you held 10+ minutes and half your loss is minted back to you as MULL. Every position's max profit is reserved at open, so winners are always paid instantly, and the contract has no owner and no upgrade path. It is for perp traders who want a venue that can't stiff winners or be changed under them, and for LPs who want fee and loss income they can actually withdraw.

=== Why did you decide to build this, and why build it now? (max 1000) ===
In October 2026 a loss-mining perps venue launched on HyperEVM paying 100 tokens per dollar lost. Traders loved the idea, then gamed it: long there, short the same size on Hyperliquid, mint for free. The tokens had nothing behind them, winners waited in a payout queue, and the contracts were upgradeable so the owner could do anything. The idea is good, the design leaks value to whoever owns the protocol. Mulligan keeps the second chance for losers and fixes the rest: rebates are paid out of losses the house already booked, so the house always keeps at least half plus fees (fuzz-tested), winners are reserved up front, and nobody holds admin keys. It is possible now because HyperEVM exposes HyperCore prices to contracts through precompiles, so a venue can settle on Hyperliquid's own L1 prices with no outside oracle.

=== What technologies are you using? ===
Solidity + Foundry (13 tests incl. fuzz), OpenZeppelin, HyperEVM + HyperCore read precompiles (0x807 oracle, 0x806 mark), Rust + alloy (keeper/CLI), React + Vite + viem, EIP-6963 wallets, Hyperliquid API, Claude Code

=== How does your product use these chains? (max 500) ===
The contract runs on HyperEVM and prices every open, close and liquidation by calling HyperCore's oracle (0x807) and mark (0x806) precompiles, no external oracle. Traders get the worse of the two to block latency arbitrage. Markets are HyperCore perps by native index. Settlement, the MULL house token and liquidations live on HyperEVM; the Rust keeper and the app read HyperCore prices through the same precompiles on mainnet.
